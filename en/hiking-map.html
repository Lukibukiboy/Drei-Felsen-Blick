Good to know

Hiking tips for your stay

🥾 Joining the Eifelsteig

The premium Aachen–Trier trail passes just 200 m from the house. Stages towards Daun or Hillesheim make great day hikes — and the train from Gerolstein (600 m from the house) brings you back.

🔦 Pack a torch

A torch is well worth it for Buchenloch Cave on the Rock Trail — the cave is freely accessible and leads around 30 metres into the mountain.

🐺 Time the wolf feeding

The Kasselburg Loop pairs perfectly with the daily flight shows and wolf feeding at the Eagle & Wolf Park — check the times online beforehand.

Welcome

Living at the forest edge, in the heart of the Volcanic Eifel

Our house on Sonnenweg sits on the quiet edge of Gerolstein, immediately next to the Gerolstein Dolomites nature reserve. Hiking trails, climbing rocks and the Buchenloch Cave are minutes away on foot — while bakery, station and town centre are still under ten.

Both apartments are non-smoking and ideal for families, groups of friends, athletes and nature lovers.

Getting here

How to find us

🚆 By train

Gerolstein station (Cologne–Trier Eifel line) is only 600 m from the house — perfect for car-free holidays and for Eifelsteig section hikers. From the station it's a gentle 10-minute uphill walk.

🚗 By car

Gerolstein is easy to reach via the A1 motorway (exit Gerolstein/Büdesheim) or the B410. Parking is available right at the house — just a few steps with your luggage.

🕓 Check-in

Arrival from 3 pm, departure by 10:30 am — other times are often possible by arrangement. Bed linen and towels are ready and waiting for you.

Everything included, no hidden costs

€80–120 per night per apartment — including final cleaning, visitor's tax, electricity, water and heating.
